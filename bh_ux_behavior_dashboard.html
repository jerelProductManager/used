Insight summary

auto-generated from the loaded data

Conversion funnel

How far participants get through the eight journey steps, and where they drop off. A big drop between two steps is where the flow is losing people.

Friction signals by step

Rage clicks, dead clicks, JS errors, idle stalls and back-navigation — attributed to the step where they happened. Taller stacks = rougher steps.

Median time on step

Typical dwell per step (median across sessions). Unexpectedly long dwell can signal hesitation or confusion; very short can signal skimming.

Search behavior — how people found their item

Did participants click a type-ahead suggestion or press Enter? Heavy Enter use can mean the suggestions weren't trusted or discoverable.

Median scroll depth by step

How far down each step people scrolled. Low depth on a long step can mean key content sat below the fold.

Ease-of-use & hesitation signals

Behaviors that hint at indecision or added effort across all sessions.

Friction hotspots — most-clicked dead & rage targets

Elements people clicked that did nothing (dead) or hammered repeatedly (rage). Prime candidates for a fix.

Where it happened — screens & clicks

Each journey screen with the clicks that landed on it. Red pins = rage clicks, amber = dead clicks, faint blue = normal clicks. Open the live screen to experience where friction occurred.

Sessions

One row per participant. Friction score weights rage & errors heaviest. Sort by clicking a column.
